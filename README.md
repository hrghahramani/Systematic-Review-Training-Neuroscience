# Systematic Review Training

## Overview

This repository documents my completed practical training in systematic review methodology and evidence synthesis within neuroscience and clinical research.

The training focused on developing practical skills in study design, research question development, systematic literature searching, reference management, screening, data extraction, critical appraisal, and risk-of-bias assessment.

## Training Objectives

- Understand and distinguish major clinical and research study designs
- Develop research questions using PICO/PECO frameworks
- Identify key concepts and terminology for research questions
- Develop systematic literature search strategies
- Use controlled vocabulary, MeSH, Emtree, and free-text keywords
- Search and retrieve literature from major biomedical and multidisciplinary databases
- Manage, organize, and deduplicate references using EndNote
- Apply predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria
- Conduct title/abstract and full-text screening using Rayyan
- Apply PRISMA principles to systematic review workflows
- Design and perform structured data extraction
- Conduct critical appraisal using JBI checklists
- Assess methodological limitations and risk of bias

## Training Assignments

### Assignment 01 — Study Design & Research Question Development

Training in identifying and distinguishing major clinical research designs, including case reports, case series, cross-sectional studies, case-control studies, cohort studies, and randomized controlled trials.

Developed research questions using PICO/PECO frameworks and identified key concepts for neuroscience literature searches.

### Assignment 02 — Systematic Literature Search & Reference Management

Practical training in developing structured database search strategies using Boolean operators, controlled vocabulary, MeSH, Emtree, synonyms, alternative terms, and related keywords.

Conducted searches across PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, and PsycINFO, followed by reference organization, deduplication, and management using EndNote.

### Assignment 03 — Screening & Systematic Review Workflow

Practical training in importing references from EndNote into Rayyan and applying predefined eligibility criteria.

Conducted title/abstract and full-text screening, documented inclusion and exclusion decisions, and practiced PRISMA-based systematic review workflow documentation.

### Assignment 04 — Data Extraction

Designed structured data extraction sheets for selected neuroscience research articles.

Extracted key study characteristics, participant information, study methodology, interventions/exposures, outcomes, and relevant findings.

### Assignment 05 — Critical Appraisal & Risk of Bias

Identified the methodological design of selected studies and applied appropriate JBI critical appraisal checklists.

Evaluated methodological limitations and assessed the overall risk of bias for selected articles.
